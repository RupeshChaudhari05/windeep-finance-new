2. Fine logic — what existed before (and what was wrong)

A fine rule is stored in fine_rules. Your active rule is "Late Payment Fine": initial + per-day, ₹100 + ₹10/day, 10 days grace, effective from 16-Feb-2026, applies to loans and savings, with no maximum cap. The daily cron (cli/cron daily) and the "Run Auto Fine Job" button call Fine_model::run_late_fine_job(), which creates one fine per overdue EMI or savings due.

3. Fine logic — how it works now

3.1 The formula (one place: Fine_model::calculate_fine_amount)

TypeFineExample (grace 10 days)
FixedFine amount, once₹100 from day 11
Percentage% of the EMI / savings due amount, once2% of ₹5,000 = ₹100
Initial + Per DayInitial fine on the first late day + per-day amount for each further day. If the initial is 0: per-day × late days.Your rule: day 11 = ₹100, day 15 = ₹140, day 40 = ₹390
SlabAmount of the band the late days fall in (slab_config JSON)1–30 days ₹100, 31–60 days ₹250

"Late days" are counted after the grace period. A maximum cap, if set, always applies. The formula for your active rule is unchanged (₹100 + ₹10/day), so nothing changes for members. Fines → Rules now shows a live preview of this formula while you type.

3.2 When a fine is charged

  1. Only for EMIs / savings dues that fell due on or after the rule's "Effective From" date. Old arrears are never fined retroactively.
  2. Unpaid, overdue and part-paid EMIs of active loans, and dues of active savings accounts.
  3. One fine per EMI. The rule is locked to the fine when it is created.
  4. The fine grows daily only while the EMI is unpaid. When the EMI is paid, the fine is frozen at the payment date.
  5. A recalculation never goes below what was already paid or waived. Waived fines are never touched again.
  6. If a rule is switched off, open fines under it stop growing.
  7. Skipped, waived, interest-only and force-closed (cancelled) EMIs are not fined.

3.3 Rule versions

Before

Editing a rule overwrote it. History was lost, and the start-date jump left a month with no rule at all.

Now

If the new "Effective From" date is later, the old rule is closed the day before and a new version (e.g. FINE-LOAN-01-V2) starts. EMIs due earlier keep the old terms. The rule list shows "until dd-mm-yyyy" for old versions.

3.4 One rule screen + Preview

  • Fines → Rules is the only rule editor. Settings → Fine Rules redirects there, and the broken modal on the Settings tab was removed. The tab still lists the rules, with links.
  • New Preview (dry run) button: runs the whole job inside a database transaction that is rolled back. It shows every fine that would be created or changed, with an Excel download. Nothing is saved.
  • After "Run Auto Fine Job", the message shows new fines, recalculated fines, the amount added and the items still within grace.

3.5 Collecting fines

  • Bank mapping / passbook import: the amount is spread over the member's open fines, the chosen fine first, then the oldest. The bank date is kept and every receipt is posted to the ledger (Cash/Bank Dr, Fine Income Cr). Each fine gets its own mapping row, so a reversal restores each fine exactly.
  • If the amount is more than the member's open fines, mapping stops with a clear message, and the passbook preview flags it. The extra must go to savings or another head instead of disappearing.
  • Waive and waiver approval work again.

6. Excel design (every report)

  • Banner: company name (from Settings), report title, period, and "generated on / by".
  • KPI strip: the same key figures as the screen cards.
  • Header row: dark blue with white bold text. Rows are zebra-striped, with borders. The header is frozen and a filter is on every column.
  • Amounts are real numbers (#,##0.00, negatives in red), so they can be summed. Dates are real Excel dates (dd-mm-yyyy). Phone, account and loan numbers are kept as text, so there is no 9.8E+09 and leading zeros stay.
  • TOTAL row uses SUBTOTAL(109,…), so totals follow your filter.
  • Extra sheets where useful, e.g. "Summary by type" or "By product".
  • Print-ready: A4, fit to page width, landscape for wide reports, header row repeated, footer "Page x of y".
  • Safe: cell text starting with =, + or @ is neutralised (no formula injection).

Code: application/libraries/Excel_report.php. A report is one "spec" (filters, KPIs, columns, rows), and the same spec draws the screen (views/admin/reports/report_view.php) and the Excel, so the two can never disagree. Adding a new report is about 30 lines.
